# AutoFish

Automatically reels your fishing rod.

## Settings

- **AutoDisable** — Automatically disable the module after a successful catch.
- **AutoSwitch** — Switches to the fishing rod automatically.
- **CastDelay** — The delay before casting the rod again.
- **Mode** (Enum): `Normal`, `Particle`, `Sound`.

## Commands

- `autofish`
- `autofish <setting> [value]`
