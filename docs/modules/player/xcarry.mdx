# XCarry

Hold items in your crafting table slots.

## Settings

- **Cancel** — Cancel packets that clear crafting slots.
- **ShiftClick** — Allow shift-clicking items into crafting slots while XCarry is active.
- **ArmorSlots** — Keep items in armor slots even when they would normally be removed.

## Commands

- `xcarry`
- `xcarry <setting> [value]`
