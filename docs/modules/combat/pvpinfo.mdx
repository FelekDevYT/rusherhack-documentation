# PvpInfo

Sends you messages with info relating to pvp.

## Settings

- **Potions** (Category)
    - **StrengthDetect** — Notify when a nearby player gains the Strength effect.
    - **SelfRunOut** (Sub-category) — Notify when your own effects expire:
        - `Strength`, `Absorption`
- **Pearls** — Notify when a player throws an ender pearl.
- **VisualRange** (Category)
    - **Sound** — Play a sound when a player enters your visual range.
    - **IgnoreFriends** — Do not notify for players on your friends list.
- **TotemPops** — Notify when a nearby player's totem pops.
- **SelfPops** — Notify when your own totem pops.
- **32k Detect** — Notify when a player is using a "32k" (super-enchanted) weapon.

## Commands

- `pvpinfo`
- `pvpinfo <setting> [value]`
