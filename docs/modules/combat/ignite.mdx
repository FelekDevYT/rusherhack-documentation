# Ignite

Light players up on fire with a flint and steel or fire charge.

## Settings

- **Reach** — The maximum distance to ignite targets.
- **Delay** — The delay between ignition attempts.
- **Swap** — Automatically swap to a flint and steel or fire charge.
- **Rotate** (Category)
    - **Speed** — Speed of rotation towards the target.
- **Targets** (Category) — *Configure the entity types to target:*
    - **Players** (Sub-category): `FriendProtect`, `PrioritizeEnemies` (EnemiesOnly), `IgnoreCreative`.
    - **Hostiles** — Target hostile mobs.
    - **Passives** (Sub-category): `IgnoreTamed`, `IgnoreNamed`.
    - **Neutrals** — Target neutral mobs.
    - **Vehicles** — Target boats and minecarts.

## Commands

- `ignite`
- `ignite <setting> [value]`
