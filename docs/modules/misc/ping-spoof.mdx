# PingSpoof

Spoof your ping.

## Settings

- **Mode** (Enum) — The method of spoofing:
    - `Spoof`, `Delay`
- **DelayMode** (Enum) — Direction of the delay:
    - `Out` — Delay outgoing packets.
    - `In` — Delay incoming packets.
- **Delay** — The amount of delay to apply (in milliseconds).

## Commands

- `pingspoof`
- `pingspoof <setting> [value]`
