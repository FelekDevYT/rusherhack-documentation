# AntiAFK

Do things to prevent afk kick.

## Settings

- **Delay** — The delay between performing AFK actions.
- **FocusedCheck** — Only perform actions if the Minecraft window is not focused.
- **ActivityCheck** (Category)
    - **Delay** — How long to wait for player inactivity before starting AntiAFK.
- **Jump** — Jump periodically.
- **Swing** — Swing your arm periodically.
- **Rotate** — Rotate your head periodically.
- **Sneak** — Sneak and unsneak periodically.
- **Move** — Move slightly back and forth.
- **/stats** — Automatically send the /stats command.

## Commands

- `antiafk`
- `antiafk <setting> [value]`
