# AutoShear

Automatically shears mobs.

## Settings

- **Range** — The maximum distance to reach and shear mobs.
- **AutoSwap** — Automatically switches to shears in your hotbar.
- **Rotate** — Rotates your head towards the mob being sheared.
- **Render** — Visualizes the shearing process or target.
- **Targets** (Category) — *Choose which entities to shear:*
    - **Sheep** — Automatically shear wool from sheep.
    - **MushroomCows** — Automatically shear mushrooms from Mooshrooms.
    - **SnowGolems** — Automatically shear pumpkins from snow golems.

## Commands

- `autoshear`
- `autoshear <setting> [value]`
