# ArmorAlert

Alerts you and other players when your or someone's armor durability is low.

## Settings

- **Render** — Visual representation of the low armor alert.
- **Notification** (Category)
    - **Clientside** — Send notifications only to yourself.
- **Self** — Alert when your own armor is low.
- **Others** — Alert when other players' armor is low.
- **Friends** — Alert when friends' armor is low.
- **NoLongerLow Alert** — Send a notification when armor is repaired/swapped and no longer low.
- **Threshold** — The durability percentage to trigger the alert.

## Commands

- `armoralert`
- `armoralert <setting> [value]`
