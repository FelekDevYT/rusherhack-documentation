# EntityControl

Ride entities without saddles and modify their speed.

## Settings

- **Control** — Allows you to control entities without a saddle.
- **HorseJump** — Allows you to control horse jump height.
- **BoatFixYaw** — Locks the boat's rotation to your camera.

## Commands

- `entitycontrol`
- `entitycontrol <setting> [value]`
