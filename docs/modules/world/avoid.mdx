# Avoid

Avoid dangerous stuff.

## Settings

- **Fire** — Prevent walking into fire.
- **Cactus** — Prevent walking into cacti.
- **Web** — Prevent walking into cobwebs.
- **Portal** — Prevent walking into portals.
- **Endermen** (Enum) — *Avoid looking into the eyes of endermen:*
    - `Off`, `Avoid`, `Aggravate`
- **Unloaded** — Prevent walking into unloaded chunks.

## Commands

- `avoid`
- `avoid <setting> [value]`
