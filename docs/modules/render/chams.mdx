# Chams

Change the way entity models are rendered, allowing you to see them through walls.

## Settings

- **Glint** — *Render the enchantment glint over the model.*
- **Fill Settings** (Category)
    - **ColorMode** (Enum): `Static`, `Custom`.
    - **Color** — *The color of the fill.*
    - **Alpha** — *The transparency of the fill.*
- **Entities** (Category) — *Configure which entity types to apply Chams to:*
    - **Players** (Self), **Hostiles**, **Passives**, **Neutrals**, **Vehicles**, **Items**, **Projectiles**, **EndCrystals**, **Others**.

## Commands

- `chams`
- `chams <setting> [value]`
