# Zoom

Zooms your camera in, similar to Optifine.

## Settings

- **Usage** (Enum) — *Activation mode:*
    - `Hold`, `Toggle`
- **Zoom** — *The amount of zoom to apply.*
- **Smooth** — *Enable smooth zooming animation.*
- **Reset** — *Reset zoom level when disabled.*
- **Scroll** — *Allow using the scroll wheel to change zoom level.*
- **Cinematic** — *Enable cinematic camera movement while zoomed.*

## Commands

- `zoom`
- `zoom <setting> [value]`
