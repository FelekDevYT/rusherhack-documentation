# FreeLook

Allows you to look around freely without changing your moving direction.

## Settings

- **Usage** (Enum) — *How the free look is activated:*
    - `Hold`, `Toggle`
- **Camera** (Enum) — *Which perspective to use:*
    - `First Person`, `Third Person`
- **Smooth** — *Enable smooth camera movement.*
- **OnlyPitch** — *Only allow vertical camera movement.*

## Commands

- `freelook`
- `freelook <setting> [value]`
