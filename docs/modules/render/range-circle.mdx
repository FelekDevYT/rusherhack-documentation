# RangeCircle

Draws a circle at your feet indicating your hit range.

## Settings

- **Radius** — *The size of the circle.*
- **Thickness** — *Width of the circle line.*
- **Morph** — *Animate the circle shape.*
- **Others** (Category)
    - **Radius** — *Show range circles for other players.*
- **Color** — *Color of the circle.*
- **RainbowMode** (Enum): `Solid`, `Dynamic`.

## Commands

- `rangecircle`
- `rangecircle <setting> [value]`
