# Direction

Displays the direction you are currently facing.

## Settings

- **Color** — The color of the direction text.
- **Axis** (Enum): `X`, `Y`.
- **Label** (Category)
    - **Color** — Color of the labels.
- **Colon** — Toggle the colon after the label.
- **Yaw** — Display your current yaw rotation.
- **Pitch** — Display your current pitch rotation.
- **Facing** — Display the cardinal direction (North, South, etc.).
